# GTFO Coon Rapids
### Get The Flock Out: Flock Surveillance Accountability
### Coon Rapids, MN

I wanted to see how my local municipality (Coon Rapids, MN) was using its Flock camera system. So in April 2026 I submitted a FOIA request for the event logs from the implementation of the system to the date of my request in April.

[Here is my analysis](https://github.com/condorairlines/GTFO_CR/blob/main/analysis.ipynb)

For me, and seemingly for many in the United States, Flock cameras are the last straw. As a Millennial, I've grown up on the internet and seen companies like Google, Meta, and Amazon exponentially explode and invade our lives. More and more our data is harvested for God-knows-what reasons, which it's later uncovered are reasons not so salutary, laudable, or good. All this data harvesting seems to be good for is enriching the asset-owning class while telling us all stories amounting to "don't worry, it'll eventually be good for you! Really!" 

As the father of a soon-to-be second grader, and as a professional Data Analyst, I want to do something so my daughter and her friends can enjoy learning, growing, and playing without their data being harvested to manipulate and nudge them toward ends they might not really want. But of course, on my way to drop my daughter off at school what do I drive past?

Flock cameras.

Not only are companies profiling my daughter via school devices and home devices; they now can add time-stamped travel patterns to their ever-growing dataset. Get enough data and the inferences to be drawn are significant. Be a multi-billion dollar company with scalable compute infrastructure to run machine learning models on that dataset, and that's straight dangerous. Share that with a government that could arbitrarily decide who is an undesireable and worthy of tracking, and that's Orwellian.

I've grown up in an economic landscape that said work hard and it'll pay off. I did work hard and those companies decided they didn't want to make it pay off. Instead, they want to extract *even more* by gathering data on our every action, both online and now out in the world on the road. I'm done with it. I want some control back in my life, and I want my daughter and her friends to be able to be left alone by these companies, to not constantly be observed and tracked and profiled and monitored. 

They say it's for safety. But the only danger I feel is from these companies and what they are free to do with their power and all this data on us. Additionally, they sell this power to our local, state, and federal governments, which are often run by geriatrics who don't understand these technologies. I don't feel safe with this technology in their hands either.

I don't trust companies like Flock because they have proven they cannot be trusted, at least in their current forms and extractive business practices. And I don't trust the governments who uncritically buy the marketing narratives sold to them and deploy these technologies, because the temptations to misuse these tools are built into the technology themselves. No tool is neutral.

I want myself and my neghbors to be left alone to live our lives.

So Flock can GTFO.
